Our Story

From the heart of
Limpopo.

Ntate Seya Peanut Butter was born from a simple conviction: that the finest peanut butter shouldn't come from a factory in some distant city. It should come from right here — from the people who grow the peanuts, who know the soil, who understand what real food tastes like.

Crafted under Seya Foods in Matsikitsane, Acornhoek, every jar carries the pride of Bushbuckridge. We use only pure roasted peanuts and salt — because that's all you need when you start with quality.

Affordable. Nutritious. Delicious. Great taste that fits every home.
